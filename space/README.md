# SoilSight

Upload photos of a soil sample on a lab tray and get a gravel / sand / fines estimate with a calibrated range,
a review flag when the model is unsure, the most similar lab-tested samples as evidence, and a grain-size heatmap.
Trained on Photogranulometry v1.0 (Plante St-Cyr, Duhaime, Dubé & Grenier, 2025; doi:10.20383/103.01316; CC BY 4.0).
